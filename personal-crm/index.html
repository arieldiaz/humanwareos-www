01 — One loop

You live the relationship. Your agents keep the threads.

  1. 01Connect

    Contacts, calendar, and email stay where they are.

  2. 02Notice

    Agents find promises, important dates, changes, and open loops.

  3. 03Confirm

    Nothing sensitive becomes memory until you approve it.

  4. 04Act

    Ask who needs attention and get a few choices with reasons.

02 — Relationship software should not feel like surveillance

The person is not a lead. The relationship is not a score.

Proposed, not presumed.Agents show their source and confidence. You decide what becomes durable.

Private, even in multiplayer.Inviting someone into a shared space never exposes your private notes about them.

Useful without lock-in.Your source systems remain Apple and Google. HumanwareOS coordinates them.
